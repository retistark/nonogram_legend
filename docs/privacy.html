Nonogram Legend Privacy Policy (English) Effective date: November 9, 2026 Nonogram Legend (the "App", developer: RetiStark) is a nonogram picture logic puzzle game. The App only handles the information needed to run the game and does not use external analytics tools. It offers optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) that you choose to watch. 1. Information we collect and how we use it Stored only on your device: game progress, solved pictures, coins and hints, achievements, check-ins, stats and settings (language, input mode, vibration, etc.). If you don't sign in, this information never leaves your device. If you sign in (optional): when you sign in with email or Google, your account identifier and email address are used for authentication. Your game progress (solved pictures, records, coins and hints, achievements and check-ins) is saved on our server (Supabase) so you can continue on multiple devices. If you join rankings, the hall of fame or friend battles (optional): the nickname you choose, scores, completion times and mistake counts are stored on the server, and your nickname and records are shown to other users. Please don't use your real name in your nickname. 2. Ads (Google AdMob) The App shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one to receive a hint or auto-fill time. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address (approximate location) and device/app information. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device settings (Google → Ads). 3. Device permissions Internet: used for sign-in, sync, rankings, friend battles and ads. Vibration: used for input and clear effects; can be turned off in Settings. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads. 4. Sharing with third parties We do not sell your information. We use Supabase (cloud storage) and Google Sign-In for login and data storage, and Google AdMob for ads; their privacy policies also apply. Your game records and nickname are not provided for advertising purposes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). 5. Account and data deletion requests How to delete the account and data of Nonogram Legend (developer: RetiStark): How to request: from the email you used to sign in, send an email to retiguy@gmail.com with the subject "Nonogram Legend account deletion request". We will verify your identity, delete your data within 7 business days and reply to you. Data that is deleted: account information (email and identifier), game progress saved in the cloud, coin, achievement and check-in records, and ranking, hall of fame and battle records (including your nickname). Deleted data cannot be recovered. Delete only some data: you can also ask by the same email to delete only specific data, such as ranking records, without deleting your account. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Friend battle records are deleted automatically after 30 days. 6. Children The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. 7. Contact Privacy questions and deletion requests: retiguy@gmail.com 8. Changes If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page.
